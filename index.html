The Seasonal Special

Flowcrate Founder · Builder

Dig deeper. Save what you find. Follow the thread.

A music discovery app for DJs and the people who dig. Start from a DJ you trust or a set you love, follow the connections outward, and save what you find into crates you can actually use.

What I did

Product
Defined the core loop: discover, save, organize, explore connections. Scoped what ships and what waits, and wrote the plans before the big builds.
Build
Designed and built it end to end: sign-in and password recovery, DJ and artist following, a personal library, crates, CSV export, and the privacy and terms pages.
Data
Connected tracks to the sets and DJs that played them, so you can see where a record has been played. Enriches tracks with BPM, key, genre and label.
Process
Runs it like a product team of one: staging before production, a release checklist for every ship, a written decision log, and automated tests covering the code and the browser.

Ingredients React · TypeScript · TanStack · Tailwind · Supabase · Vercel
